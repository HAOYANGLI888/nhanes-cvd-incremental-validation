# Statistical Analysis Plan v2 Locked

This plan locks the NHANES-only reconstruction before final modeling. The study estimates cross-sectional identification and incremental information for prevalent self-reported CVD. Model families are Elastic Net logistic regression as primary and XGBoost as secondary nonlinear sensitivity. Performance will be evaluated by discrimination, calibration, log loss, Brier score, and incremental metrics in common target samples. Survey-weighted association analyses remain separate from prediction modeling.
